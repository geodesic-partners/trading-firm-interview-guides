# How trading firms interview

One guide per firm: what each says about its own process, what candidates report, and the pay stated in its adverts. Each guide keeps those apart and links its sources.

- [Jane Street interview questions](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/jane-street-interview-questions?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): How Jane Street interviews traders, from its own guidance: phone rounds, then a final covering probability, markets and coding. Six worked practice problems.
- [Optiver interview questions](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/optiver-interview-questions?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Optiver's trader process from its own recruitment pages: online assessments, a recruiter interview and a final day. Practice problems and the 80 in 8 format.
- [Citadel quant research interview](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/citadel-quant-interview?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Citadel's campus quant research process from its own page: a coding interview first, then three to five technical rounds. Probability and statistics practice.
- [IMC interview questions](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/imc-interview-questions?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): IMC's trader recruitment from its own pages: online assessment, behavioural and technical interviews, an assessment day, a programming test. Practice problems.
- [Five Rings interview](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/five-rings-interview?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): The Five Rings quant trader interview as candidates describe it: a timed mental maths and estimation screen, trader rounds on probability and games, a final.
- [Two Sigma interview questions](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/two-sigma-interview-questions?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): How Two Sigma interviews quant researchers, from its own pages: video rounds, technical and behavioural. Intern pay by degree and six worked practice problems.
- [D. E. Shaw interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/de-shaw-interview-process?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): D. E. Shaw's hiring steps from its own interviewing page, the quant analyst intern pay and housing it states, when to apply, and six worked practice problems.
- [DRW interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/drw-interview-process?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): DRW's hiring steps from its graduate page: CV, a timed technical challenge, a Zoom interview, a two-day final. Stated intern pay and six practice problems.
- [Hudson River Trading interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/hudson-river-trading-interview?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): HRT's interview stages from its own blog: a timed take-home, phone rounds on systems and programming, and a full onsite day. Six worked practice problems.
- [SIG interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/sig-interview-process?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Susquehanna's graduate trader process from its own advert: online test, phone interviews, a trader interview on the floor. Stated intern pay and six problems.
- [G-Research interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/g-research-interview-process?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): G-Research's quant research hiring stages from its own careers pages: technical, behavioural and management interviews, the aptitude test, and six problems.
- [Flow Traders interview process](https://geodesicpartners.co.uk/flow-traders-interview?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo): Flow Traders' graduate trader process from its careers pages: CV and cover letter, a 75-question maths test, recruiter, team and final rounds, six problems.
